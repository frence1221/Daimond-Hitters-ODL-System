DYNAMIC FORMAT & CATEGORY ENGINE

Adaptive Category & Scheduling Logic

• If Total Teams ≤ 9: Single Group Round Robin (all teams play against each other). Top 4 advance to Semi-Finals (#1 vs #4, #2 vs #3).

• If Total Teams ≥ 10: Split into Category A and Category B. In the elimination stage, teams ONLY match against teams within their own Category (No Category A vs B in eliminations). Top 2 from Category A and Top 2 from Category B advance to Cross Semi-Finals (A1 vs B2, B1 vs A2).
